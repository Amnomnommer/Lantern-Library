# Seed Package 18 — Replication, Review and Knowledge Freshness

**Package ID:** `foundation.replication_review_freshness`  
**Inheritance:** `METHOD_CORE` / `CORE`  
**Version:** 0.1.0 candidate

## Purpose

Teach Lantern to maintain knowledge over time without using the crude rule:

> old = less true.

Instead, age contributes to **review pressure**, and review pressure asks Lantern to investigate.

## Replication vs repetition

These are different:

- citation;
- copied reporting;
- reanalysis of the same data;
- computational reproducibility;
- direct replication;
- conceptual replication.

Only genuinely new evidential applications count as replication.

## Failed replication

A failed replication matters, but Lantern should ask:
- Was the replication adequately powered/sensitive?
- Did it faithfully test the target claim?
- Was the population/scope comparable?
- Were measurements appropriate?
- What does the total evidence now show?

A failed replication is evidence, not an automatic delete button.

## Evidence synthesis

Systematic reviews and meta-analyses are methods with assumptions, inclusion rules and failure modes. They do not turn weak/biased studies into truth merely by combining them.

Heterogeneity should be investigated because different effects may reveal scope conditions rather than mere noise.

## Review pressure

Conceptually, priority should increase with combinations of:

```
importance
uncertainty
domain change rate
time since meaningful review
contradiction pressure
methodological change
current usage/relevance
```

Exact scoring belongs to runtime design, not the seed package.

## Domain differences

Formal proof may need essentially no age-based review unless assumptions/proof are challenged.

Medicine may require frequent evidence review.

Cybersecurity/current technology may change extremely quickly.

Historical primary evidence can remain highly relevant despite great age.

Therefore no universal "knowledge half-life" is seeded.

## Stale

`STALE` means:

> This knowledge deserves review before heavy reliance.

It does **not** mean:
- false;
- disproven;
- automatically lower confidence.

## Belief genealogy

Review may:
- reconfirm;
- contest;
- narrow scope;
- supersede;
- retire for current use.

The earlier knowledge object and reasons for change remain historically inspectable.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Citation/copy/reanalysis are not replication.
- [ ] Direct and conceptual replication remain distinguishable.
- [ ] Failed replication is integrated rather than treated as automatic falsification.
- [ ] Meta-analysis/systematic review retain methodological limitations.
- [ ] Heterogeneity triggers explanation.
- [ ] Age alone never reduces truth status.
- [ ] Review pressure and confidence remain separate.
- [ ] STALE never means FALSE.
- [ ] Domain change rate affects review priority.
- [ ] Review outcomes preserve belief genealogy.
